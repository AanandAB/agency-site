Key Engineering Highlights

📐

Haversine Spatial Verification

Computes true spherical distance from store origin coordinates (`11.8314722, 75.5517778`). Delivery is free under 7 km or for orders exceeding ₹2,000. All distances and cart values are strictly re-verified on the server side to eliminate tampering.

🌐

Complete Bilingual Localization

Full English and Malayalam translation layer persisted in localStorage. Every product title, colour tag, banner notification, and checkout receipt switches seamlessly without page reload.

📲

WhatsApp Order Stream

Instant order creation: saving order details to Cloudflare D1 while simultaneously piping formatted order confirmations directly to WhatsApp with phone verification and live tracking links (`/track`).

✨

GSAP & Lenis Motion Choreography

Smooth anchor-aware Lenis scrolling with rotating pookalam floral motifs, countdown timer to Thiruvonam, and micro-interactions optimized for high conversion.
