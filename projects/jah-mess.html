The Problem: Chaos, Food Wastage, and Reconciliation Nightmares

University hostels and community dining messes traditionally rely on paper coupons or manual ledger books. This leads to rampant systemic breakdowns:

  • Counterfeited and Passed Coupons: Paper slips are shared among non-paying guests, resulting in budget deficits.
  • Blind Kitchen Prep: Chefs have zero foresight into how many students will eat on any given evening, resulting in tens of kilograms of untouched food dumped into waste bins nightly.
  • Mess-Cut Calculation Disputes: Calculating pro-rated daily food cuts manually for hundreds of residents causes bitter administrative friction every billing cycle.

The Engineering Solution

JAH Mess transforms the operation into an automated, transparent, high-efficiency machine:

  • Cryptographically Nonced Tokens: Student smartphone passes generate rotating time-sensitive QR signatures preventing screenshots or resale.
  • Kitchen Demand Dashboard: Cooks see exact forecasted dining headcounts 4 hours in advance based on registered meal cuts, curbing raw ingredient over-preparation.
  • Automated Billing & Ledger: Pro-rated rebate credits are automatically credited to the student ledger when leave requests are pre-approved.
